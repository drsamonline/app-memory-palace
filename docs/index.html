the walkRecall happens in the order you placed them — and a wrong answer is shown back in its own room, not just marked wrong. “At the table you should have had ‘apple’.”

The order is the method

A right thing at the wrong spot is still a miss, because the method is the walk and not the list.

What it refuses

It will not become a flashcard app: no schedule, no ease factor, no due list. And it will not quietly drop an item to make room.
